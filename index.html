Background

Analytical Precision & Strategic Clarity

Sara is a seasoned professional with over four years of experience spanning Big Four transaction advisory, corporate finance, and BI engineering across EMEA and MENA regions. She helps private equity investors, corporate leadership, family offices, and fast-scaling tech companies dissect complex commercial structures and forecast cash flows under multi-scenario market conditions.

Her focus is deployed across three distinct advisory and technical scopes. First, delivering best-practice FP&A, FC, operational, and M&A models, encompassing advanced DCF valuations, purchase price allocations, and scenario stress-testing. Second, engineering data infrastructure by transforming disparate ERP extracts and high-volume transactional repositories into automated, audit-ready dataflows. Third, building strategic executive dashboards which translate commercial metrics into interactive, automated Power BI environments for portfolio oversight.

Sara is grounded in the belief that simplicity is the highest form of precision. Her philosophy is built on zero-template engineering because no two business models or capital structures are identical.
